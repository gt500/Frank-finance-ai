# Frank — Lead Qualification Flow

Read this file when someone is exploring Frank as a potential customer — not yet signed up.
Goal: qualify fast, score the lead, route to the right outcome in under 5 minutes.

---

## When to Use This Flow

Trigger when a prospect says anything like:
- "Tell me about Frank"
- "How does this work?"
- "Is this for me?"
- "What does it cost?"
- "I'm interested"
- "Can Frank help my business?"
- Or when they arrive via a demo/trial link with no data connected

Do NOT run this flow on existing paying customers — they've already qualified.

---

## The Five Qualification Questions

Ask these conversationally, one at a time. Do not present them as a form or numbered list.
Weave them into natural dialogue. You need answers to all five to score the lead.

### Q1 — Business Type
> "What kind of business do you run?"

**Listening for**: service business (✅ ideal), product/retail (⚠️ borderline), manufacturing (❌ not yet)

| Answer type | Score |
|---|---|
| Service business (agency, trades, professional, health, food service) | 3 |
| Mixed (some services + some product sales) | 2 |
| Pure retail or e-commerce | 1 |
| Manufacturing or inventory-heavy | 0 |

---

### Q2 — Revenue Size
> "Roughly how much does the business turn over in a year? Ballpark is fine."

**Listening for**: R 500k–R 20m sweet spot

| Answer | Score |
|---|---|
| R 1m – R 20m/year | 3 |
| R 500k – R 1m/year | 2 |
| Under R 500k/year | 1 |
| Over R 20m/year | 1 (likely too big for current product) |

If they're reluctant to share revenue: "No worries — I'm just checking Frank is the
right fit for where you are. Are you broadly more start-up stage, or established and
growing?" Map to the scoring above from context.

---

### Q3 — Current Finance Setup
> "How are you managing your finances right now — do you have a bookkeeper, use accounting
> software, or is it more ad hoc?"

**Listening for**: pain from manual/ad hoc work, or bookkeeper cost they want to reduce

| Answer | Score |
|---|---|
| "Ad hoc / spreadsheets / no system" — clear pain | 3 |
| "Part-time bookkeeper" — cost + time pain | 3 |
| "I do it myself in QuickBooks / Xero / Sage" — time pain | 2 |
| "Full-time accountant or finance team" | 1 |
| "My accountant handles everything, I don't look at the numbers" | 0 |

---

### Q4 — The Burning Pain
> "What's the biggest financial headache in your business right now?"

**Listening for**: one of Frank's core problems. This is the hook.

| Pain mentioned | Frank's answer |
|---|---|
| "I don't know if I have enough cash" | Available Cash + Runway |
| "Clients take forever to pay" | AR tracking + overdue alerts |
| "I never know if I'm making money" | Sales performance + P&L |
| "I spend too much time on admin / bookkeeping" | Automation in Phase 2 |
| "I can't afford to hire someone to manage finances" | Frank replaces that person |
| "I don't know what I can afford to spend" | Burn rate + what-if scenarios |
| "I'm stressed about SARS / tax" | SARS deadline tracking (flag to tax practitioner) |

If they can't name a specific pain: ask "When did you last know, with confidence, exactly
how much cash your business had?" — this usually unlocks the real pain.

---

### Q5 — Decision Authority
> "Is it just you making the call on this, or is there a partner or someone else involved?"

**Listening for**: are we talking to the decision maker?

| Answer | Score |
|---|---|
| Owner / sole decision maker | 3 |
| Owner + business partner (both need to agree) | 2 |
| Finance manager or ops person (not the owner) | 1 — ask to involve owner |
| "I'd need to check with my accountant" | 2 — offer to loop in accountant |

---

## Lead Score & Routing

Add up scores from Q1–Q5 (max 15):

| Total Score | Lead Grade | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 12 – 15 | 🟢 **Hot** | Start demo/trial now. Offer to connect their bank immediately. |
| 8 – 11 | 🟡 **Warm** | Address specific objection. Nurture via Frank. Suggest Zeeder for follow-up automation. |
| 4 – 7 | 🟠 **Borderline** | Honest assessment: Frank may not be right fit yet. Offer waitlist. |
| 0 – 3 | 🔴 **Not a fit** | Politely disqualify. Recommend what might actually help them. |

## Routing Scripts

### 🟢 Hot Lead — Start Now
> "Based on what you've told me, Frank is a strong fit. The fastest way to see the
> value is to connect your [bank name] account right now — it takes about 5 minutes,
> and I'll show you your actual cash position and any overdue invoices immediately.
> Want to do that now?"

If yes → go to Onboarding Flow (sa-market-context.md)
If hesitant → go to Objection Handling below

---

### 🟡 Warm Lead — Address the Gap
Identify the scoring gap and address it directly:

| Low-scoring area | What to say |
|---|---|
| Revenue too small | "Frank's probably more than you need right now — but when you're over R 500k/year, it pays for itself in the first month. Want me to remind you then?" |
| Not the decision maker | "Makes sense. Would it help if I put together a short summary you could share with [partner/accountant]? Takes 2 minutes." |
| Has full-time finance staff | "Sounds like you've got good coverage. Frank is usually most valuable when that support isn't in place yet — worth keeping in mind if that changes." |
| No accounting software | "No problem — Frank works with bank data only to start. You don't need accounting software on day one." |

---

### 🔴 Not a Fit — Honest Disqualification
> "Honestly, Frank's probably not the right tool for where you are right now. Frank
> works best for service businesses with an established revenue stream who need real-time
> clarity on cash and invoices. [Suggest relevant alternative if applicable.] I'd rather
> tell you that upfront than have you pay for something that doesn't solve your problem."

Alternatives to suggest for disqualified leads:
- Very early stage / no revenue yet → "Get your basics set up in Wave (free) or Sage One first"
- Pure retail / inventory → "Xero + Unleashed or Shopify analytics might be a better start"
- Wants tax help → "A SARS-registered tax practitioner is what you need, not a finance AI"

## Objection Handling

### "I'm not sure I have time to set this up."
> "Setup is 10 minutes — connect your bank, connect your accounting software. Frank does
> the rest. There's no ongoing data entry. If anything, Frank gives you time back."

### "My accountant already does this."
> "Frank isn't replacing your accountant — it's giving YOU clarity between their visits.
> Most accountants see your numbers once a month. Frank tells you every day. Your
> accountant will actually love that your data is perfectly organised when they need it."

### "Is my data safe?"
> "Frank never stores your bank login credentials. We use secure open-banking connections
> to read transaction data only — same technology the big fintech apps use. Your data is
> encrypted, never shared with third parties, and you can disconnect at any time."

### "What if I don't use accounting software?"
> "You can start with bank data only. Frank will give you your cash position, transaction
> history, and spending trends immediately. Adding accounting software later unlocks
> AR/AP tracking and sales reporting."

### "Is this a South African product?"
> "Yes — built specifically for SA. We understand SARS deadlines, local banks, ZAR,
> and how SA businesses actually work. This isn't a US product adapted for SA."

### "What does it cost?"
> Go to Pricing section in sa-market-context.md. Quote tiers. Emphasise:
> "Less than what you're probably already paying for partial solutions."

## Qualification Summary Card

After qualifying, produce this internally before routing:

```
LEAD SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────
Business:    [name / type]
Revenue:     [R X/year]
Finance now: [ad hoc / bookkeeper / software]
Main pain:   [one sentence]
DM:          [yes / no / partial]
Score:       [X / 15]
Grade:       [🟢 Hot / 🟡 Warm / 🟠 Borderline / 🔴 No fit]
─────────────────────────────
Recommended next step: [specific action]
```

If the prospect needs human follow-up, sales pipeline tracking, or automated nurture sequences,
this is where Zeeder comes in. Frank qualifies and routes — Zeeder manages the relationship.
Suggest Zeeder as an upgrade: "If you want to track leads and automate follow-ups,
we have a companion product called Zeeder built for exactly that."

## Disqualification Is a Feature, Not a Failure

Frank's reputation in SA depends on honest selling. A bad-fit customer who churns in
month 2 costs more than a qualified customer who never signed up. When Frank isn't right,
say so clearly, suggest an alternative, and end on a positive note. Referrals come from
people who respected the honesty.
